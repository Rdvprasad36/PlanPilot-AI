# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - PlanPilot AI

## 1. Vision & Pitch
PlanPilot AI is a GenAI-powered planning assistant that helps users organize weddings, birthdays, and community events with budgets, timelines, vendor checklists, and personalized ideas in under a minute.

## 2. Target Users
- Priya, 28, engaged: wedding plan within budget and family constraints.
- Arjun, 35, parent: stress-free birthday plan for 25 kids.
- Meera, 45, community volunteer: low-budget neighbourhood festival plan with volunteers and sponsors.

## 3. Core Requirements
- Guided Wizard + Refine Panel.
- 3 event types with type-specific templates and prompts.
- Budget allocator with category percentages, planned vs actual, and overspend alerts.
- Backwards-planned timeline from event date with overdue states and ICS export.
- Per-category vendor checklist with status, quote, contact, and notes.
- Personalized ideas grouped by theme, food, entertainment, decor, activities, and budget savers.
- Offline fallback mode with deterministic templates.
